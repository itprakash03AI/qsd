AWS portal logins: how they work and what is proven

Status at 2026-11-05 01:40 EST, for release d8c734ddd (hotfix 2). The grid in section 4 has run on this code on Docker. Hotfix 1 (8f8d3272a) does not contain these fixes. The CI suite (41,107 passed, 0 failed) and the frontend suite (2,981 of 2,981) passed on this code.

1 · The recommendation for today's demo

On each S3 connection, pick your Vault AD account under System Account (AWS Portal). It does not depend on any person's password, and on Docker it worked on every road in section 4, with values matching. This needs hotfix 2: on hotfix 1, feeds with a System Account fail on the feed worker.

  • A person's password is held only in the memory of the pod they logged in on. It is never written to disk. A pod restart clears it, and their session survives, so they stay logged in with no password held. That is why the window opened after your deploy.
  • Admin → AWS Portal supplies its values, such as the portal address, only while its Enabled switch is on. With it off, a connection needs its own address under "Override global portal settings".
  • A wrong password the portal refuses is not sent again for 5 minutes, so queries cannot lock an AD account. A network failure does not count, and a corrected password is tried at once.
  • Personal with nothing saved works only on the pod you logged in on. Test, browse and SQL work. Reports on the reports pod, published APIs and feeds cannot use your password, and say so.
  • Old connections do not change by themselves. A connection on Personal with an old account left on it stays the person's own login until someone picks an account on the form.

Login A's three refusals are expected. The password is held on the pod the person logged in to, so those pods have none. On a real report the message is: "This AWS connection logs in with each person's own password, and none is held here. Log in again, or choose a System Account on the connection." A published API answers: "This API's connection could not log in to AWS. Its owner needs to check the connection's account."
